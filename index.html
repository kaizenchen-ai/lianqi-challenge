📖 象棋玩法與規則說明

🔥 連棋 (連吃)
🎯 暗棋 (傳統)

⚡ 連棋模式（連吃 Combo 爽快版）特點：

  • 同階互吃：相等的同階級也可以互相吃！（例如士吃士、車吃車、兵吃兵）
  • 士不能吃將：黑士可吃紅仕與仕以下各階（仕相車馬炮兵），但絕對不能吃紅帥/將！
  • 卒吃帥、兵吃將：黑卒可以吃紅帥，紅兵可以吃黑將！（小卒立大功）
  • 無限連吃（Combo）：吃子後若新位置周圍還有合法可吃之敵棋，可以不限次數連續一直吃下去！直到把周圍敵棋吃完或主動點選完成！
  • 衝暗棋（盲吃連吃）：連吃或平時可直接攻擊相鄰未翻開暗棋：
    • 翻出可吃之敵棋（如黑士翻出紅仕/相/車/馬/炮/兵，或黑卒翻出紅帥）➡️ 成功吃下並可繼續連吃！
    • 翻出自己同色棋（如黑士翻出黑棋）➡️ 揭曉友軍，吃不下並結束連吃換手。
    • 翻出比自己大的敵棋（如黑士翻出紅帥，或黑馬翻出紅車）➡️ 揭曉敵棋，吃不下並結束連吃換手。
  • 炮跳吃暗棋：炮隔一子跳吃，無論目標已翻開或未翻開皆可跳打！

⚡ Lianqi Mode (Continuous Combo) Highlights:

  • Equal-Rank Capture: Pieces of the exact same rank can capture each other! (Advisor takes Advisor, Chariot takes Chariot, Pawn takes Pawn).
  • Advisor Cannot Take King: Advisors (仕/士) can capture Advisors, Elephants, Chariots, Horses, Cannons, and Pawns, but CANNOT capture the King/General (帥/將)!
  • Pawn Takes King: Black Pawns (卒) can capture Red King (帥); Red Pawns (兵) can capture Black General (將)!
  • Infinite Continuous Combo: After capturing a piece, if there are adjacent enemy or dark pieces you can capture, you can keep capturing continuously with no limit until you finish or end turn!
  • Blind Capture (Dark Piece Attack): You can directly attack adjacent unrevealed dark pieces:
    • Revealed enemy piece is equal or lower rank (e.g. Advisor hits Elephant, or Pawn hits King) ➡️ Captured & continue Combo!
    • Revealed piece is friendly piece ➡️ Revealed! Cannot capture & turn ends.
    • Revealed enemy piece is higher rank (e.g. Advisor hits King) ➡️ Revealed! Cannot capture & turn ends.
  • Cannon Screen Attack: Cannons jump over exactly one piece (screen) to capture any piece, including unrevealed dark pieces!

🎯 傳統經典暗棋特點：

  • 一回合一步：每回合嚴格只能進行一個動作（翻一顆暗棋、移動一步、或吃一顆敵棋）。
  • 吃子即換手：吃子後立即結束回合換對方走，不可連吃。
  • 炮翻山跳吃明棋：同一直線隔一子跳吃已翻開之敵方明棋。
  • 階級尊卑：帥將 ＞ 仕士 ＞ 相象 ＞ 俥車 ＞ 傌馬 ＞ 炮包 ＞ 兵卒（兵卒剋帥將，同階互吃）。
  • 困斃與和棋：無暗棋可翻且無子可走者判負；連續 30 回合無吃子判定和局。

🎯 Traditional Banqi (Classic Dark Chess) Rules:

  • One Move Per Turn: In each turn, you can strictly perform only ONE action: reveal 1 dark piece, move 1 step, or capture 1 piece.
  • End Turn on Capture: Capturing immediately ends your turn. No continuous combo.
  • Cannon Screen Jump: Cannons jump over exactly one piece (screen) to capture revealed enemy pieces in a straight line.
  • Piece Hierarchy: King ＞ Advisor ＞ Elephant ＞ Chariot ＞ Horse ＞ Cannon ＞ Pawn (Pawn captures King; equal ranks can capture each other).
  • Stalemate & Draw: Player with no legal moves loses; 30 consecutive moves without capture results in a draw.
